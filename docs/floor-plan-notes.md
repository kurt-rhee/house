# Floor plan design notes

## Kitchen and dining opening

- Rounded arch, 1,400 mm wide, with no door.
- Arch crown at 2,180 mm and springline at 1,780 mm.

## Kitchen cabinetry and stove wall

- Refrigerator with an oak surround and upper cabinets.
- Recessed upper cabinets at the microwave with full-height soapstone.
- Plaster above the soapstone on the stove wall, with no shelf.
- Arched warm stone, 1,250 mm wide to match the hood.
- Plaster hood with rounded vertical sides and a flat top and bottom.

## Kitchen windows and lighting

- Sink awning window, 2,389 × 561 mm, with a 1,476 mm sill and 2,037 mm head. Include a concealed shade and 1 ft at each end.
- Round window, Ø450 mm, with its center at 1,756.5 mm and aligned with the sink.
- Ceiling pendant with a 560 mm paper lantern.
