# Floor plan design notes

## Finished interior dimensions on A-02

A-01 supplies the original finished wall-to-wall dimensions. A-02 carries those dimensions through the proposed layout and rounds the resulting spans to the nearest inch.

| Proposed room | A-01 reference | Finished span on A-02 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kitchen | Original kitchen | 10′ 2″ × 13′ 9″ |
| Bedroom 3 | Original second living room | 12′ 1″ × 10′ 11″ |
| ADU bedroom | 11′ 5″ × 9′ 6″ bedroom and adjoining 7′ 4″ × 5′ 2″ space | 19′ 1″ × 9′ 6″ overall |
| Bathroom 1 | Original 6′ 5″ × 8′ 0″ hall bathroom and adjoining closets | 5′ 11″ × 8′ 0″ |
| Bathroom 2 | Same hall bathroom and closet envelope | 4′ 11″ × 8′ 0″ |

- The kitchen and Bedroom 3 use the original spans at the marked sections. The proposed opening and closet detail change along their shared boundary.
- The ADU bedroom's overall width adds the two original clear widths and the removed partition's nominal 4⅛″ thickness. The 9′ 6″ depth is the original bedroom depth. The retained bathroom makes the floor area smaller than the overall rectangle.
- The two baths share an 11′ 2″ interior span between the bedroom-side partitions, aligned with the A-01 hallway width, and retain the original bathroom's 8′ depth. The proposed divider center is about 73¼″ from the Bathroom 1 side. Each room's clear width deducts half of the nominal 4⅛″ divider thickness.
- Starting with CAD revision 58, A-02 and the app measure the same finished wall faces. The two bathroom spans round to the nearest inch. The opening widths and wall thicknesses retain their design values.
